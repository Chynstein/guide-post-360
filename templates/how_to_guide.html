GuidePost360 How-To Guide

Quick reference for navigating and using the campus map

Getting Started

Signing In

  1. On the dashboard, click the Safety Personnel button
  2. You will be taken directly to the map
Tip: If you need elevator-accessible routes, check the "I need elevator access" box before signing in.

First-Time Agreement

The first time you open the map in a browser session, a brief privacy notice will appear. Click I Agree to continue. This only appears once per session.

Switching Buildings & Floors

  • Use the dropdown menu in the top-left corner of the map
  • Click it to see all available buildings and floors
  • Select the location you want to view

You can also switch floors by clicking a staircase or elevator on the map — see the Help tab for details on floor switching.

Finding Rooms

Using Room Finder (Quick Search)

  1. Open the Pathfinder panel:
    • Mobile: Swipe up from the bottom, or tap the Pathfinder tab
    • Desktop: Click the Pathfinder tab on the left side
  2. Scroll down to the Room Finder section
  3. Type a room number, location name, or related term
  4. Select from the suggestions that appear
  5. Click Find — the map will zoom to that location and highlight it with a yellow star

Tapping on the Map

Click or tap any room label directly on the map to open an info panel popup showing:

  • Room name and room number
  • Floor / building location
  • Description (if one has been added)
  • Images (if any have been added)
  • Directions From / Directions To buttons to quickly start navigation

Search Tips

  • You can search by room number or location names (e.g., "bathroom", "cafeteria", "gym")
  • Many locations have aliases — for example, searching "lunchroom" will find the Commons
  • If you can't find something, try a related term

Getting Directions

Using the Pathfinder

  1. Open the Pathfinder panel:
    • Mobile: Swipe up from the bottom, or tap the Pathfinder tab
    • Desktop: Click the Pathfinder tab on the left side
  2. In the From field, type your starting location
  3. In the To field, type your destination
  4. Click Find Route

Reading Your Route

  • A colored path appears on the map showing your route with a green dot moving along the path to indicate movement direction
  • Per-floor instructions appear in the header bar at the top of the screen
  • Each step in the header bar is clickable — tap any step to jump back to it
  • If the route crosses floors, you'll see which stairs or elevator to use

Quick Directions from a Room

You can also start directions directly from any room's info panel popup:

  1. Tap a room label on the map to open the info panel
  2. Click Directions From to set it as your starting point, or Directions To to set it as your destination
  3. Fill in the other field and click Find Route

Elevator Mode

If you need to avoid stairs:

  1. Check the "I need elevator access" box on the dashboard before signing in, OR
  2. Toggle Elevator Mode in the Pathfinder panel at any time

When enabled, all routes will use elevators instead of stairs. If no elevator access is possible for a given route, a warning message will appear.

Route Tips

  • Click Clear to remove the current route and start over
  • You can search by room number or common location names in both From and To fields

Floor Switching & Troubleshooting

Switching Floors via Staircases

When you click on a staircase or elevator on the map:

  1. An info panel popup appears with floor information
  2. Click the Go Upstairs or Go Downstairs button
  3. The map automatically switches to that floor and opens the corresponding staircase's info panel on the new floor
Tip: You can also switch buildings/floors at any time using the dropdown menu in the top-left corner.

Troubleshooting

Map not loading?

  • Refresh the page
  • Make sure you're connected to the internet

Can't find a room?

  • Try searching by a related term (e.g., "gym" instead of "gymnasium")
  • Check if you're looking on the correct building or floor

Route not showing?

  • Make sure both the From and To fields are filled in
  • Clear the route and try again
